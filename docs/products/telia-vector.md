# Telia Vector (Carrier Edition)

**Telia Vector** is a managed on-premises edge compute solution delivered in commercial partnership between **Telia** and **Velocitee** (`velocit.ee`).

## The Solution for Nordic & Baltic SMEs

Small and medium-sized enterprises (law firms, medical clinics, accounting practices) are caught in the SME IT dilemma:
1. **Sovereignty Mandate:** Client confidentiality and European data privacy laws (GDPR, EU NIS2) penalize off-site unencrypted cloud storage.
2. **Complexity Gap:** Deploying on-premises servers requires specialized systems engineering contracts costing €150+/hour.
3. **Underutilized Edge:** The Telia business router sitting in the SME office is capable of acting as an edge orchestrator.

Telia Vector activates the **Telia X2 (Technicolor DGA4330)** router as a zero-touch cryptographic gateway:
- **Port 4 (Vector Port):** Unprovisioned hardware connects to Port 4 and enters an isolated quarantine network.
- **One-Click Claim:** The SME administrator claims the detected node via Telia Self-Service (*Iseteenindus*).
- **Automated Deployment:** In under 3 minutes, the node provisions itself, initializes LUKS2 encryption, and launches private office workspaces (e.g., sovereign document vault, accounting database).
- **Hybrid Backup:** Encrypted snapshots sync automatically to Telia's Tier-3 Data Center in Tallinn.

## Commercial Partnership Model

- **ISV Partnership:** Velocitee provides the software platform under a recurring license model; Telia acts as the exclusive carrier distribution channel.
- **ARPU Expansion:** Telia bundles Telia Vector as an add-on subscription (€89–€119/month), driving high-margin revenue and zero churn risk.
