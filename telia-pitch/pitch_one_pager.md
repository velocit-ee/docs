## Verbal Pitch Script (2 Minutes)

### 1. The Hook (30 seconds)
"Hello judges. Meet Kati. Kati runs a 12-person legal and accounting firm here in Tallinn. She has a major problem: her clients trust her with confidential financial data, NDAs, and audit logs. Under GDPR and upcoming NIS2 compliance, storing unencrypted or offshore cloud copies is a massive liability. 

Kati wants a private server in her office. But if she buys hardware, who configures it? Who sets up the RAID, the networking, the firewall, and the private cloud stack? Nobody. Kati doesn’t have a sysadmin, and local IT consultancies charge €150 an hour.

Right now, she's trapped between expensive, complex hardware and hyperscaler cloud bills."

### 2. The Solution & Telia's Role (45 seconds)
"What if Kati could unbox a server, plug it directly into her existing Telia Business Router, and have a production-ready, sovereign private cloud operational in 3 minutes—with zero IT intervention?

That is what **Velocitee** does. By embedding an ultra-lightweight zero-touch bootstrap agent directly at the router edge—running comfortably in under 50 MB of RAM on Telia’s existing Zyxel CPE hardware class—the Telia router becomes a cryptographic provisioning gateway.

When new hardware is plugged in, it enters an isolated quarantine network. Kati sees a notification in her Telia Business Portal: *'New node detected. Click to claim.'* One click, and the machine netboots, configures itself, and brings up a secure, private business workspace."

### 3. The Business Model for Telia (45 seconds)
"Why is this a massive win for Telia?
1. **ARPU Growth:** Connectivity is commoditized. By bundling **Telia Private Edge** as a managed add-on, Telia elevates a €45/month internet bill into a €120/month managed infrastructure subscription.
2. **The Ultimate Churn Killer:** Once an enterprise runs their day-to-day legal archives and local apps through this gateway, they will never switch away from Telia.
3. **ISV Partnership, Not Telia Overhead:** Telia doesn’t need to hire an R&D team to build and maintain metal provisioning software. Velocitee operates as the specialized software vendor (like Microsoft 365 or F-Secure), licensing the platform to Telia on a recurring revenue split.

Telia supplies the connection and hardware; Velocitee powers the zero-touch sovereign cloud."
